\begin{center}
    {\Large\bfseries Inclined Plane Force Problems}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item A \(25\,\mathrm{kg}\) luggage trolley rolls down a straight airport access ramp inclined at \(20^\circ\) to the horizontal. Rolling resistance is a constant \(20\,\mathrm{N}\) up the ramp. Take \(g=9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) throughout.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Calculate the normal reaction from the ramp on the trolley.
        \item Calculate the trolley's acceleration down the ramp.
    \end{enumerate}

    \item A \(40\,\mathrm{kg}\) equipment trolley is being hauled up a loading ramp inclined at \(15^\circ\) to the horizontal. A cable pulls it with a force of \(180\,\mathrm{N}\) parallel to the ramp. Rolling resistance is \(25\,\mathrm{N}\) down the ramp.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Calculate the normal reaction from the ramp on the trolley.
        \item Calculate the trolley's acceleration up the ramp.
    \end{enumerate}

    \item A \(30\,\mathrm{kg}\) equipment sled is being lowered down a hillside by a rope held by a rescue team uphill. The rope's tension is \(50\,\mathrm{N}\), acting parallel to the slope. The normal reaction from the ground is \(255\,\mathrm{N}\), and the sled accelerates down the slope at \(0.80\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\). Friction acts up the slope.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Calculate the angle of the hillside to the horizontal.
        \item Calculate the magnitude of the frictional force.
    \end{enumerate}

    \item A \(50\,\mathrm{kg}\) delivery crate moves up a loading ramp inclined at \(10^\circ\) to the horizontal. A winch pulls parallel to the ramp, while a hold-down strap presses the crate perpendicular to the ramp. The normal reaction on the crate is \(510\,\mathrm{N}\), and resistance to its motion is \(35\,\mathrm{N}\) down the ramp. It accelerates up the ramp at \(0.80\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\).
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Calculate the force exerted by the hold-down strap on the crate.
        \item Calculate the pulling force exerted by the winch.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large\bfseries Answers}
\end{center}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{(a)} \(230\,\mathrm{N}\) \quad \textbf{(b)} \(2.56\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) down the ramp
    \item \textbf{(a)} \(379\,\mathrm{N}\) \quad \textbf{(b)} \(1.34\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) up the ramp
    \item \textbf{(a)} \(29.9^\circ\) \quad \textbf{(b)} \(72.9\,\mathrm{N}\) up the slope
    \item \textbf{(a)} \(27.0\,\mathrm{N}\) into the ramp \quad \textbf{(b)} \(160\,\mathrm{N}\) up the ramp
\end{enumerate}
